THE FRAMEWORK

Four stages. Each one necessary.

None of them optional if the goal is the moment a learner stops reciting a vulnerability class and starts understanding why it works.

D

Deconstruct

Understand the system before you touch it. What it's built from, what it's trusting, and why. Ten to fifteen minutes, scoped tight to the vulnerability.

A

Attack

Demonstration, guided replication, then a new target. The exploit is evidence that understanding is there. It is not the understanding itself.

R

Remediate

The patched version runs beside the vulnerable one. Same attack, same learner. This time it fails. A pentester is supposed to fail.

E

Exchange

The learner switches sides. The attacker reads their own attack in the logs. This is where "now I got it" happens most often.
