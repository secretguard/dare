Planning assistant

DARE is my framework, and this is where I hand you how to run it. Choose the certification programme you teach and get a whole-programme DARE plan, or plan a single module. Or switch to a single topic and build one session. Either way you get a Deconstruct–Attack–Remediate–Exchange outline sized to the exact minutes you have, with the specific lab from labs.sarathg.me to run it on. Every block is written and tested, never generated. You are not rewriting your syllabus; you are resequencing what you already deliver so learners actually understand it.

Pick a topic and a sector, set your minutes, then build the outline.
