DARE for certification programs

Most certification programs are fixed syllabi taught to a clock: CEH, Security+, OSCP, CPENT, SC-200, AWS Security and the rest. They are strong on coverage and, almost without exception, thin on two things, watching the fix actually work and seeing the attack from the other side. That is precisely the gap DARE closes. You do not rewrite the syllabus. You resequence what you already deliver and add the perspective the curriculum left out.

How DARE fits any program. Pick one objective from the certification you teach. Spend a few minutes on Deconstruct so the learner can explain the system before touching it. Run the Attack the exam already expects. Then add the two stages the syllabus skips: Remediate, where the same attack fails against a patched version, and Exchange, where the learner sees their attack from the defender’s seat. Same content, same exam objectives, resequenced into understanding. The mappings below are a starting point; the planning assistant will build any of them to the exact time you have.
